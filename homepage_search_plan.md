# Homepage V2: "The Universal Interface"

## 1. Core Shift
*   **From:** "Identity Infrastructure" (Passive, storage-focused)
*   **To:** "The Ultimate Question Engine" (Active, utility-focused)
*   **Key Insight:** Search is the primary intent. Identity is the *byproduct* of answering and interacting.

## 2. Strategic Narrative
Why use Qbase instead of X?
*   **vs. Google:** No SEO spam. Just answers.
*   **vs. Perplexity/ChatGPT:** Humans are in the loop. You get *wisdom* and *consensus*, not just probabilistic text generation.
*   **vs. Reddit/Quora:** Structured data. No digging through threads. AI synthesizes the human chaos instantly.

**The Hook:** "The only place where AI speed meets Human wisdom."

## 3. Section Breakdown

### Section 1: The Hero - "The Universal Input"
*   **Visual:** A clean, centered, beautiful input bar. It pulses gently.
*   **Headline:** "Ask the Collective Mind."
*   **Subhead:** "Tap into a global knowledge graph of humans and AI. No links, just truth."
*   **Interaction:**
    *   Auto-complete simulation: User sees typed questions like "What is the best way to..."
    *   Result: Shows "3 Sources: AI (Claude), 428 Humans, Your Context".
*   **CTA:** "Start Asking" (Focuses input).

### Section 2: The "Trinity" of Answers
*   **Concept:** Qbase gives you 3 layers of truth.
*   **Visual:** A Venn diagram or 3-column split that merges.
    1.  **Artificial Intelligence:** "Instant synthesis from top models."
    2.  **Human Consensus:** "Real data from verified humans. '80% of founders recommend X'."
    3.  **Your Context:** "Personalized to you. (We know you prefer open source, so here is Y)."

### Section 3: The Graph - "One Question, One Node"
*   **Problem:** The internet is fragmented. 10,000 Reddit threads asking the same thing.
*   **Solution:** The Canonical Node.
*   **Visual:** A chaotic web of messy lines (The Internet) coalescing into a single, glowing, perfect node (Qbase).
*   **Copy:** "Stop searching through noise. We structure the world's wisdom into a single, queryable graph."

### Section 4: Discovery - "Explore the Zeitgeist"
*   **Content:** "Trending Questions" but visualized as data.
*   **Cards:**
    *   "Is AI Conscious?" -> 45% Yes / 55% No.
    *   "Best React Framework?" -> 60% Next.js.
*   **Action:** "Add your vote." (This is the growth loop).

### Section 5: The "Why" (Footer/Bottom)
*   **Tagline:** "The Search Engine that pays you back." (Hinting at the crypto/tokenomics aspect gently).
*   **Links:** "For Agents", "For Humans", "Manifesto".

## 4. Implementation Notes
*   **Search Bar:** Needs to feel incredibly premium. Glassmorphism, inner shadows, glow effects.
*   **Animations:** Focus on the *convergence* of data. Particles flying into the search bar.
*   **Dark Mode:** Essential for the "Search Engine" vibe (like Perplexity).
